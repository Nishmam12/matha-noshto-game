# Session 18 — Walk cycle wired, standing holds the last frame

## Goal

Replace the six-way **idle** player art with the six-way **walk** art delivered in
`assets/player_new/`, and make a standing player hold whichever frame the stride stopped on rather
than snap to a rest pose. Design record: [[Character Switch Plan]] §6.

## Why now — the bake was already broken

The six `idle_*.png` files had been deleted from the working tree while `tools/bake.ps1` still named
them in `$script:CharSheets`. The next bake of any kind would have thrown `missing char sheet`. The
game still built only because `src/art_data.h` is committed and already contained them. Every art
change in the project was blocked behind this.

## The measurement that reversed a decision

[[Character Switch Plan]] §D2 chose one shared cycle for standing and moving **because** §1a had
measured the idle art as a bob: `opaque_x` identical across all 8 frames, both feet planted, no leg
alternation. The walk sheets were measured the same way and are a genuine stride, so that reasoning
does not carry:

- 384×64, pitch 48, 8 frames — **identical geometry to idle**, so the baker's dimension check took
  them unchanged and no slicer work was needed.
- **8 of 8 frames unique in every sheet.** Idle repeated frame 0 at frame 7 and was baked as seven;
  these cannot be.
- Diagonals split into **two runs across 2–3 scanlines on frames 0/3/4/7**, closing to one run on
  1/2/5/6 — the two-contact structure of a real eight-frame cycle.
- **0 key-magenta hits** against all four shades in `$script:KeyMagenta`. Re-checked, not assumed.

- `Project Status.md`, `design/Character Switch Plan.md` — records updated.

### The trap this session produced

**The first version of the `--move-test` check passed against a deliberately broken build.** It read
`anim` straight after the 8-direction sweep, and 240 steps per direction is a whole multiple of the
40-step wrap cycle at `TICK_DT`, so the sweep leaves `anim` at exactly 0 — which is also the value
the broken behaviour produces. Every assertion below it held vacuously.

Rebuilt with 7 extra input steps (coprime with the 40-step cycle) and a **live vacuity guard** that
fails if the phase is not non-zero before anything is concluded from it. Re-broken and re-run: now
fails `0.116667 -> 0.000000` on every seed. This is the fourth test in this codebase found asserting
nothing, and the only reason it was caught is that breaking the fix on purpose is a required step.

## Still owed — needs a human

- **The standing pose is completely static** — no breath, no sway, by design. Whether it reads as
  intentional or as a hang is a judgement nobody has made.
- **Five of six facings remain unphotographed.** They are covered by the row-distinctness check, and
  the walk `down` frame is confirmed at spawn.
- **The cycle is proven by test, not by eye.** `--autoplay` is wall-clock driven and the camera
  deadzone moves the player off screen centre, so an animated strip would need a debug flag that
  does not exist.
