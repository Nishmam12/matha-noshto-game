## Worn paths (Task B)

Buildings sit a few tiles apart; the paths between them are what make the village read as lived-in. The constraint that shaped the whole design: `game_init` must not reorder its RNG stream, or every fixed seed's world (and every selftest baseline) shifts. So `place_paths()` is RNG-free by construction:

- `World.path[WORLD_H][WORLD_W]` — a render-only byte flag, never solid, never read by pathfinding or the stream.
- Each building's **door strip**: 2–5 tiles out from its front corner (`+x/+y`), hashed from `b->variant*2654435761u + (b->x*17 + b->y*31 + b->w*7 + b->h*13)` so it is stable, seed-independent, and per-building distinct.
- **Connecting lanes**: Bresenham runs between front corners of houses whose centres are within `PATH_CLUSTER` (24 tiles) of each other — the same neighbourhood metric `buildings_assign_regions` already uses, so a path never spans the whole map.
- Excludes solid tiles and `SURF_RIVER`/`SURF_OCEAN` deck tiles (two of the first 30 seeds failed when a lane crossed a bridge-cleared river tile — the river surface is non-solid, so "not solid" alone was not enough).

Render: a warm dirt fill `0x8c7a4f` in `tile_colour` (before the river branch), three dark clod marks `0x68583a` in `tile_detail`, and the grass second-scatter is gated off path tiles so the lane reads smooth next to tufted grass. Gated behind `g_suppress_paths` in selftest builds.

Verification: `--path-test --seeds 30` PASS (mean 120 path tiles/world), negative control PASS (paths suppressed → 0 tiles, 22/22 houses doorless).

## Ground marks (Task C)

Three new mark kinds, each gated to the surface it belongs to and drawn purely from the tile hash (deterministic, no RNG):

- **Tufts** (`tile_tuft`): three rising blades on inland grass, the rightmost lit — reads as blades of grass, not sprinkles.
- **Mortar** (`tile_mortar`): two horizontal courses plus one staggered joint on dressed stone (`terr < 0`, never the sea).
- **Pebbles** (`tile_pebbles`): four pale ovals on the shoreline ring (`sea_dist == 1`), bright on purpose — a beach is the brightest strip in the overworld.
- All fogged with the existing marks and dream-shifted in the dream realm.

The `--ground-test` harness renders a crafted world (all sea except one rock, one beach-ring, one grass tile) three times — marks on, marks on again for determinism, marks off — then asserts: deterministic, drew at all, **no leak onto the sea**, each kind reached its surface, and a negative control (a stray pixel stamped at a sea tile's centre) is flagged.

The confinement check had to be the *inner half-diamond* of each sea tile, not the full one: a tuft's blades legitimately rise a few px over their own tile's edge into the diamond of the sea tile above, so the full-diamond test failed correct renders at seeds 777/4242. The half-sized region is at least six px from every land edge and no mark of ours can reach it. (An earlier draft also skipped solid tiles — but the sea is solid in the crafted world too, so the control stamp went undetected until the check keyed on `SURF_OCEAN` instead.)

Verification: `--ground-test` PASS at 10/10 seeds (1, 13, 777, 2024, 4242, 9001, 12345, 65535, 999983, 1234567), control PASS each time. Full suite re-run all-green. Release 777,728 bytes (+1,536 over the Task-A baseline, 662,272 headroom under the 1,440,000 ship target).
